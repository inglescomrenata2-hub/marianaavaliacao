Reading Comprehension
Read and answer — logistics & workplace context
Maria has worked in logistics for six years. She started out as a warehouse assistant and worked her way up to operations coordinator. Her job involves managing inbound and outbound consignments, coordinating with carriers, and making sure time-sensitive deliveries reach their destination without delays.

Last month, one of their main carriers had a problem with a truck, and a large freight shipment was delayed by two days. Maria had to figure out an alternative route and contact the clients directly. It was stressful, but in the end, she handled it well. Her manager said her morale during difficult situations is one of her greatest strengths. Maria is likely to be promoted soon, which would be a reasonable recognition of everything she has done for the company.
